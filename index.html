How the index works

Every title gets two percentile ranks across the whole dataset: one for score, one for audience size. The underseen index is the first minus the second. A title in the 95th percentile for score but the 20th for audience scores 75; a blockbuster with a mediocre score goes negative.

Ranking by index alone surfaces obscurity for its own sake, so when you select genres the list re-ranks on index times taste match, the share of your selected genres a title carries. Percentiles are computed once on the full dataset, so filtering changes what you see, never where a title sits.

Score is a crowd average and thin vote counts are volatile, so an obscure title rated 9 by a few hundred people is a weaker signal than the index implies. Treat the quadrant as a shortlist, not a verdict.
